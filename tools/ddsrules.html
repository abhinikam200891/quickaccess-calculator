⏳ 1. Minimum Time Required (6 Months & 1 Day)

Eligibility Rule

To earn interest on a Daily Deposit account, the account must remain open for at least 6 months and 1 day.

Completed Time Status Interest Rate Result
Less than 6 Months Not Eligible 0.00% Closed too early. No interest is paid.
Exactly 6 Months & 0 Days Not Eligible 0.00% Needs 1 more day to qualify for interest.
6 Months & 1 Day or more Eligible 2.00% or 5.00% Qualifies for full interest calculation.

📈 2. Interest Rate (2% vs 5%)

Rate Slabs

The interest rate you receive depends on how long your account has been active:

Up to 1 Year (≤ 12 Months)
2.00% per year
For accounts open between 6 months 1 day and 12 months.
More than 1 Year (> 12 Months)
5.00% per year
For accounts open for 12 months and 1 day or longer.
💡 Helpful Tip: An account closed at exactly 12 months earns 2.00%. If kept for just 1 extra day (12 months and 1 day), the rate increases to 5.00% for the entire duration!

🛡️ 3. Monthly Limits & Extra 1-Day Bonus

Deposit Limits

Every account has a daily deposit limit agreed at opening (for example, ₹1,000 per day):

  • Normal Monthly Limit: Daily Limit × Number of days in that month.
  • Extra 1-Day Bonus in 30-Day Months: In months that have 30 days (April, June, September, November), the bank gives you the benefit of 31 days (Daily Limit × 31) so you can earn more interest.
  • Extra Deposits: Any money deposited above the monthly limit does not earn interest for that month.

✂️ 4. 3% Agent Commission Recovery

Deduction Rule

The bank pays a 3% commission to the daily collection agent. If you deposit extra money beyond your monthly limit, a 3% deduction is taken from your interest on that extra amount.

Applies only to the last 6 months: This deduction is only made on extra deposits made during the last 6 months before withdrawal. Extra deposits made earlier than the last 6 months are completely free of deduction (no penalty).

When Extra Deposit Was Made Is 3% Commission Deducted? Rule Explanation
Earlier than the last 6 months NO (₹0 Deduction) Older savings are protected; no commission is deducted.
During the last 6 months YES (3% on extra amount) 3% is recovered from interest (Extra Amount × 3%).

🔄 5. Missing Months Are Carried Forward Automatically

Continuous Balance

If you do not make deposits in some months, the system automatically carries your previous closing balance forward with ₹0 deposit. This ensures your monthly interest calculation continues smoothly up to the withdrawal date without any missing months.

⚖️ 6. How the Last Two Months Are Handled

Closing Rules

When you close the account, the interest for the final months is adjusted as follows:

  • Closing Month (Withdrawal Month): 0% balance — No interest is paid for the month in which the account is closed.
  • Month Before Closing: Interest is calculated on half (50%) of the balance.
  • All Earlier Months: Earn interest on the full (100%) balance.
  • Mid-term Withdrawals: If you withdrew any money before closing, that withdrawal amount is subtracted from your balance.

📐 7. Simple Interest Calculation Summary

Formula

The final interest payment is calculated in three easy steps:

Step 1: Each month's interest = (Adjusted Balance × Interest Rate) ÷ 1200
Step 2: Total Gross Interest = Add up interest from all months + opening balance interest
Step 3: Final Amount Paid = Total Gross Interest − 3% Commission (if any)
